Cigarette Equivalent

Rule of thumb from Berkeley Earth: breathing 22 µg/m³ of PM2.5 over 24 hours is roughly one cigarette. An illustration of exposure, not a medical measurement, though it is funny imagining the entire nation all coming together for a smoke

Indoors is assumed to have about 40% of the outdoor PM2.5 (a typical figure for a home with windows mostly closed), so 0% outdoors lowers the count but does not reach zero. Air-purified rooms may be lower; open windows higher.

Haze Forecast

Model forecast of the island-wide average 1-hour PSI (the mean PM2.5 of the five NEA regions, converted to PSI with NEA's breakpoints). The range is an 80% prediction interval: about 8 times in 10 the actual reading should land inside it. The model is refreshed with new satellite fire, weather and NEA data every hour. Not an official forecast.
